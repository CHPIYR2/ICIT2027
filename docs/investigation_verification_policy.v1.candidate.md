# Investigation Protocol v1 — verification policy candidate

Version: verification-policy-v1-candidate-20260925. Specification only; no B4
implementation is authorized yet. Existing B0 helper checks remain unchanged.

The deterministic verifier receives the exact B3 output, its actual retrieved
bundle and receipt, and the frozen schemas/policy. It may use no evaluation gold,
G labels, raw private topology, additional retrieval or LLM calls.

Check exact reference existence and role, view/retrieval visibility, ancestry,
mapping/asset/channel agreement, command type/COT, quality, unit, exact time order,
and the approved numeric operation/tolerances. Both mapping edges are mandatory
for same-asset N/E temporal claims. Each substantive typed proposition requires a
complete cited support set. Missing/ambiguous mapping never permits a guessed asset.

SUPPORTED: the whole typed proposition and permitted canonical interpretation are
supported and may be published unchanged. QUALIFIED: only a predefined weaker
canonical proposition is fully supported; publish that exact weaker scope plus
its reason and retained citations. INSUFFICIENT: no such permitted substantive
proposition has adequate accessible support; withhold with an explicit missing
requirement. Numeric/schema/refusal failures remain recorded, not silently repaired.

The strongest automatic cross-source statement is a supported temporal relation;
same-asset scope requires the two mappings. Do not publish execution, physical
causation, malicious intent, compromise, attribution or safe-from-absence. Q6
primitive facts are not automatically investigation-relevant: publish only what
the frozen relevance policy can check, otherwise state the limitation. Canonical
rendering cannot claim to verify arbitrary free prose; independent-of-verifier
human review must still assess all raw/final stronger text for evaluation.

reported_state_change remains optional and requires two quality-valid same-channel
strictly ordered unequal booleans; no initial values. A single state is a value.
Do not use state transitions as a mandatory question/event sufficiency gate.

Full-view eligibility, retrieved support and final recovery stay separate.
Withholding because retrieval omitted support does not establish full-view absence.
Q7's supported explanation of limitations does not establish its prohibited
guardrail propositions. Required metric inputs/disposition oracle are defined in
the metrics candidate, independently of this verifier's own labels.
